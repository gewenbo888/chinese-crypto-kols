# Global Chinese Crypto KOLs Ranking · 全球华语加密 KOL 影响力排行榜

Comprehensive ranking of the most influential crypto KOLs in the Chinese-speaking community — exchange founders, miners, DeFi builders, VCs, analysts, media, and anon Twitter voices — by Influence Score, Followers, and Signal Posts.

Bilingual (English + 中文), with province-of-origin stats on a second tab.

## Links

- **Live:** [cnkol.psyverse.fun](https://cnkol.psyverse.fun)
- **GitHub:** [github.com/gewenbo888/chinese-crypto-kols](https://github.com/gewenbo888/chinese-crypto-kols)

## Coverage

~50 KOLs across:
- Exchange founders (CZ · Yi He · Justin Sun · Star Xu · Bobby Lee · Charlie Lee …)
- Mining & hardware (Jihan Wu · Micree Zhan · Jiang Zhuoer · Discus Fish …)
- DeFi & security (Da Hongfei · Cos / Yu Xian · Sandy Peng …)
- Investors & VCs (Dovey Wan · Mable Jiang · Jet Cai · Xiao Feng …)
- Media & analysts (Colin Wu · Lao Yapi · Phyrex · Chang Jia …)
- Anons & Twitter voices (Ai Yi · 0xSun · 链上巴菲特 …)

## Stack

- Next.js 16 · React 19 · App Router (`src/app/`)
- Tailwind CSS 4
- Fully client-side ranking; no database
- Bilingual via `src/data/i18n.ts`; data in `src/data/researchers.ts`

## Disclaimer

Follower counts and influence scores are approximations compiled from public X, Weibo, and industry sources. Inclusion is descriptive, not endorsement.
